# Research and Theory

This directory consolidates research papers, proposals, and theories related to Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Latent Topology, and General Mathematics and Physics. 

These documents serve as the foundational research for the `Creative-Programming` and `Creation Suite` projects.

## Directory Structure
- **`/LLM-Latent-Topology`**: Research focusing on the geometry, topology, and kinematics of Large Language Model latent spaces (e.g., Prompt Dynamics, Hilbert Space Characteristics).
- **`/AI-MUD-Integration`**: Documents related to integrating AI into Multi-User Dungeon (MUD) games, storytelling, and world-building.
- **`/General-Physics-Math`**: Broad research on mathematics, algorithms, machine learning practices, and physics.

## Google Docs
Because native Google Docs (`.gdoc`) cannot be copied as local text files, a generated index of all related Google Docs found on the drive is available in `GoogleDocs_Index.md` with links to the original documents.
